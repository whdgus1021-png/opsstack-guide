Method

How these numbers were checked

Every price and limit on this site comes from the vendor's own pricing page or product documentation, opened on the date printed at the top of each article. Where a vendor shows two prices, we record both: the annual-billing rate and the monthly one, because "from $19.99" usually means "billed for a year, up front".

We then convert prices into a unit we can compare — cost per 1,000 operations, per seat, per client — because the sticker price is the least useful number on the page. The limits that decide whether a plan works are usually not the headline one: Make's free tier allows 1,000 operations but only two active workflows and a 15-minute minimum interval; n8n's €20 Starter plan caps a single execution at 5 minutes while the €50 Pro plan allows 40; HubSpot's free CRM includes two users, no matter how good the CRM is.

  • Promotions are labelled. HubSpot's Starter price is currently a new-customer discount against a $20 list price. We say so rather than quoting the discount as the price.
  • Prices are re-checked quarterly, and immediately when a vendor announces a repackaging — the kind that quietly turns a recurring commission into a one-off, or a per-operation plan into a credit plan.
  • No ranking is for sale. Affiliate links never change order. If a tool we earn nothing from is the better fit, it goes first and the article says why.
  • We name who a tool is wrong for. Every review has a section for the reader who should not buy it. If we cannot write one, we have not used the product enough to review it.

Found a price that has moved? Open an issue on the public repository; corrections are dated in the article.

Scope

Who this is for — and who it is not

This site is written for the person who owns operations at a studio of three to fifty people: the one who inherits "the automations", pays the software bill, and has to explain to a client why the weekly report arrived on Thursday. Typically that means an agency, a small SaaS, or a solo consultant with subcontractors.

It is not written for enterprise IT procurement. Nothing here covers SSO enforcement, SOC 2 evidence, or multi-region deployment, because the tools at this price point mostly do not offer them — and where they do (n8n Business at €667/month, Make Enterprise), the honest advice is usually to stay off them until a client contract requires it.
